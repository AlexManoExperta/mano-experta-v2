Meet Alex

One skilled hand for the whole house

Mano Experta is Alex Rascón's handyman business. Alex came up through union carpentry, and for the past 7 years he's put that training to work for homeowners and property owners across central and northern New Mexico.

Stucco cracking on a parapet, a leak on a tar and gravel roof, a room that needs sheetrock, or cabinets that need a fresh coat: instead of calling five different trades, call one. Alex shows up, explains the job straight, and does it right.
